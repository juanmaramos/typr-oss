Typr Local provides read-only access to the local production Typr database.
It requires a compatible desktop AI client and an initialized Typr OSS
database. It cannot record meetings, change notes, or connect ChatGPT web.
Results reflect stored records and can be incomplete. Check source records
before relying on an AI-generated summary.

You control which AI client receives access. Ensure you have permission to
share the meeting information you request. Your client's and provider's terms
apply to their services, accounts, and handling of returned data. See the
[plugin privacy notice](PRIVACY.md) for the plugin's data flow and controls.
The plugin itself requires no Typr account, subscription, or API key.
